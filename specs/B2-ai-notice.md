# B2 pupil AI notice

Status: approved by Bright with the team, 26 November 2026 (D-42, D-43). The data protection consultant still confirms it before the pilot (prd section 10). Any change they ask for is made here and in `src/components/common/copy.ts`.

Shown once after first sign-in (prd FR-3, `specs/B2.md` step 4). Under 80 words, plain English, calm, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.

**Title:** Before you start

**Body (68 words):**

ATLAS is a learning app. A computer program called AI reads some of your answers. It looks at what you write to see how you are thinking. It does not see your name. If you send a photo of your writing, the photo is deleted once your words are saved. The AI can make mistakes. A teacher can check its work. If something feels wrong, tell your teacher.

**Button:** I understand

What it covers, from prd section 10: the pupil is told they are dealing with an AI; no name goes to the AI; handwriting photos are deleted once the text is confirmed; a teacher can check the AI. There is no chat with the AI, so the notice does not mention one.
