# B8 Exam

Source: ATLAS V1 Build Guide, Claude Code pack tab, section 4. Exported 24 November 2026 in session S00.

```text
Read CLAUDE.md and specs/framework.md (Stage 6 and Stage 7). This is step B8: the timed exam. Plan first, domain tests first.
Domain: examTimer.ts (start, remaining time from a stored start time so closing the app does not reset it, expiry) and scoring.ts (objective scoring, totals, percentage).
Flow:
1. The exam unlocks when every segment is complete or moved on. Show rules, duration and a "Start exam" button.
2. Objective section, then theory section. Pupils can move between questions within a section. No feedback of any kind until submission.
3. Timer always visible but calm. At zero, play the stop-work sound and auto-submit.
4. On submit: score objective items locally; send each theory answer to ai-mark-theory. Borderline marks show "Being checked by a teacher" until an admin approves.
5. Results: total score, then every question where marks were lost, with what was credited, what was missing, the suggested answer, and a link back to the segment video.
6. Two buttons: "Revise weak segments" and "Retake now". A retake uses Paper B.
Done when: a paper auto-submits at zero even if the app was closed and reopened; results match a hand-marked copy of the same answers.
```
